# Centre outreach & backlink playbook

Goal: listed centres link to their HyperDog profile and correct their data.

## Who to contact

1. Centres already in the directory (highest conversion).
2. Centres that submitted via `/list-your-centre`.
3. Hydrotherapy / canine rehab associations (directory mention or resource page).

## Email template — existing listing

**Subject:** Your centre on HyperDog Therapy (dogtherapycentres.co.uk)

Hi [Name / team],

We list **[Centre name]** on HyperDog Therapy, the UK directory at **dogtherapycentres.co.uk** (brand: HyperDog Therapy).

Your profile: **[profile URL]**

Could you:

1. Confirm the details are accurate (services, phone, website).
2. Add a link to your profile from your website “Find us” or footer (helps owners find you).

To suggest edits or add services: **[list-your-centre URL]** or reply to this email.

Thank you,  
[Your name]  
HyperDog Therapy

## Email template — new centre invite

**Subject:** Free listing — UK dog hydrotherapy directory

Hi [Name],

HyperDog Therapy lists UK dog hydrotherapy, physiotherapy and rehab centres for owners researching care. Submit free: **https://www.dogtherapycentres.co.uk/list-your-centre**

We do not replace veterinary advice; listings link to your site and Google Maps for bookings.

Best,  
[Your name]
